## The repetitive ticket problem

Up to 70% of support tickets are recurring questions: order status, refund terms, plan settings. Operators spend their time copy-pasting from policy documents while customers wait for hours.

RAG lets answers cite your documents and policies instead of generic model knowledge. {platformName} connects Knowledge Base, AI Studio, and channels so human escalation stays an explicit part of the flow.

## How it works on the platform

1. **Knowledge Base** indexes policies, FAQs, and instructions: every agent answer is grounded in a specific document fragment with a transparent source.
2. **AI Studio** defines the scenario: when to answer automatically, when to hand off to an operator, and with what context.
3. **Channels** (Telegram, website, email) feed requests into a single queue — the operator sees the full history and a drafted answer.

In practice you get predictable answers from your knowledge base, logging for every decision, and fast source updates without redeploying code.

## Quality control

Automation does not remove accountability: low-confidence answers are flagged, escalation to an operator is a designed step rather than a failure, and accuracy metrics are tracked per source so weak policies surface immediately.
