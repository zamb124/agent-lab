## The expensive subscription puzzle

A typical small-business stack is a chatbot, a knowledge base, a CRM, a messenger, and an office suite from five different vendors — glued together with integrations, duplicate accounts, and disjoint access models.

When agents, knowledge base, CRM graph, chat, and office documents share one perimeter, you avoid a patchwork of subscriptions and glue integrations. A single entry point and company model simplify security and onboarding.

## What a shared perimeter gives you

- **Shared data.** A document from Documents is indexed by Knowledge Base without exports; a client from NetWorkle is visible to the agent mid-dialogue.
- **Unified security.** One role model and audit trail across all products instead of five independent admin panels.
- **Connected scenarios.** An agent reads the knowledge base, writes a note into the NetWorkle graph, and replies to the client in Sync — inside a single flow.

## Total cost of ownership

The bundle reduces not only direct subscription fees but hidden costs: fewer integrations to maintain, one contract, a single onboarding path for employees, and predictable LLM token billing.
